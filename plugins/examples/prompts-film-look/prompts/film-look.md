Retouch the photos in Develop's filmstrip toward a warm film look.

For each photo:

1. Look at it first (`view`) and read its recipe (`get_recipe`). Note the light, the skin tones and
   any sky.
2. Ask `suggest_looks` which looks suit the photo and start from the best-scoring warm one.
3. Adapt it to this photo: keep skin natural, never clip the highlights, and lift the blacks only
   a little. Warmth lives in the colour grading (amber highlights, cool shadows) more than in the
   temperature slider.
4. Check the result with `preview` before you stop, and say in one sentence what you changed.

Change drafts only. Every change is one step the person can undo.
